## 1. DeLisa Fairweather, PhD — Mayo Clinic Florida (EDS Clinic, co-director of research)

**Why picked:** Senior/corresponding author on two different studies our site cites
on two different pages — the largest clinic-based fibromyalgia/hEDS overlap study
(n=733) and the largest sex-differences study in hEDS/HSD to date (n=2,451), both
from the Mayo Clinic Florida EDS Clinic. A lab actively publishing this much
hEDS/HSD-specific data is a strong, directly relevant link target.

**Pages referenced:**
- `sex-differences-heds-hsd-symptoms` — https://hypermobilitysupport.com/sex-differences-heds-hsd-symptoms/ (primary)
- `fibromyalgia-hypermobility-overlap-misdiagnosis` — https://hypermobilitysupport.com/fibromyalgia-hypermobility-overlap-misdiagnosis/ (secondary mention)

**Contact route:** No direct lab email surfaced in research; her Mayo Clinic Florida
faculty bio page (mayoclinic.org/biographies/fairweather-delisa-ph-d) is the
most reliable starting point — **verify a current contact address there before
sending.**

**Subject:** A quick note on the Wilson/Fairweather sex-differences paper — we cited it in detail

**Body:**

> Hi Dr. Fairweather,
>
> I run Hypermobility Support (hypermobilitysupport.com), a small, citation-heavy
> reference site for hEDS/HSD patients — I write it myself, not a clinic or a
> company, and every claim on it links back to the actual paper.

> I just finished a page on sex differences in hEDS/HSD that leans entirely on
